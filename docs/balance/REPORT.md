# Balance report

Generated by `tools/sim/run_balance.gd` (#6): 5000 seeded runs per strategy, 10 days each, same seeds for every strategy.

**The bot:** lands Super Perfect, Perfect, Good, OK and Miss equally often (20% each; a Miss lands $1.01–$3.00 off, over or under at random). It starts a pour 1.0 s after a car parks, plus up to 0.6 s per other hand-played pump. Cars take 3.0 s to drive in.
**The game:** today's numbers: 1 starting pump, $50 starting cash, rent $20 + $6 a day, a 400 L tank, fuel 70% of the pump price wholesale.

## Short days v0.30.0 (#35, 2026-10-20)

Day 1 lasts 15 s (7 to 11 AM). The HOURS branch stretches it to 22.5, 34, 45 and 60 s. A search over rent and prices (`tools/sim/tune.gd`, third and fourth passes) picked:
- **Rent:** $20 on day 1, plus $6 a day.
- **Prices:** every upgrade at half price (`shop_price_scale` 0.5).

Results:
- **Survival:** three different routes survive 68–77% of runs (hours and pumps in turn, pumps first, hours first). Hoarding survives 9.5%.
- **The economy leans on Dad's tank:** with a 100 L tank, nothing survived. The 400 L inherited tank (about $560 of free fuel) is what carries the first days, and it lasts many short days, so the fuel order barely matters early.
- **Days are thin:** only 2 to 4 cars a day. A 15 s day leaves time for about 2 cars per pump: 3 s to drive in, about 1 s to react, then a 2.5 to 10 s pour.
- **More cars per day is the real lever** (cars queue and pull in at once, or smaller orders). That's a design decision; see BACKLOG.
