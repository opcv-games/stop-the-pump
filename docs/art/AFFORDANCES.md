# Affordance brief: station kit (Phase 4)

Every asset says one thing to the player before it is pretty. Reference: Edward Hopper, *Gas* (1940).
Rule: the things you act on are saturated and lit; the world around them is muted and dark.

| Asset | What it must tell the player | How the shape and colour say it |
|---|---|---|
| **Pump** | "This is what you act on." Its number and display must read at a glance in any light. | Tall upright box, the only saturated **red** in the scene (Hopper's pumps). White globe on top that glows when pouring. Display face turned toward the camera. Big number plate. |
| **Self-service pump** (later variant) | "This one runs itself." | Same red body plus a blue card-reader box and a blue stripe: readable silhouette difference, not just colour. |
| **Car** | "A customer is waiting here." Its colour codes the customer type. | Low, long, horizontal: the opposite of the pump's vertical, so the two never blur together. Muted colours so the pumps stay the brightest thing. |
| **Station building** | Mood and place. Not interactive. | Small white clapboard box, dark roof, warm yellow windows that light up at night. Low contrast by day. |
| **Sign pole** | Mood: "a roadside gas station". | Tall thin pole with a round sign; frames the composition like Hopper's Mobilgas sign. |
| **Road** | "Cars come from here." Leads the eye from the edge of the screen to the pumps. | A strip that enters from the left and runs past the pumps. |
| **Woods** | Mood and frame. Not interactive. | Dark, dense masses of trees behind the station; deepest values in the scene, so everything in front pops. |
| **Grass / ground** | Mood. | Hopper's dry gold grass at the edge of the road; flat, quiet. |

## Light (from the day clock)
- **Morning:** low warm sun from the east, long shadows across the road.
- **Afternoon:** high flat light, bleached colours.
- **Night:** dark blue sky; the light comes from the station windows, the porch lamp and the pump globes.
- **Rain (later):** darker values, wet road reflections.

## Readability check (every render)
1. Can you count the pumps and read their numbers in a 1-second glance?
2. Is any non-interactive thing brighter or more saturated than the pumps? If yes, fix it.
3. At night, are the pumps still the first thing the eye finds?
